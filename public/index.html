About Me

I'm Yousef Mohamed El-Basuony, a Software Engineer with a strong passion for problem-solving and mathematics. I love transforming complex ideas into clean, efficient applications.

Currently, I work as a Freelance Instructor & Coach at DEPI (Round 4), helping trainees improve their technical skills, freelancing experience, and project-building journey.

I completed Level 1 training with the Digital Cubs of Egypt initiative and successfully graduated from the Mobile Application Development track at the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI).

I also completed a Frontend Development internship at the National Telecommunication Institute (NTI), where I gained hands-on experience in frontend technologies and modern web development.

My technical interests include Mobile Application Development using Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP/KMM), Frontend Development, and Backend Development using .NET. I continuously work on strengthening my skills by building projects and exploring modern technologies.

I have also participated in several technology events and communities, including a Google-sponsored event in Egypt, which helped broaden my perspective on innovation and the tech industry.
